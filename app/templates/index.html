How a report is made

  1. Sample faces

    Faces are cut out of evenly spaced frames, so the model looks at the face and not the background.

  2. Score each frame

    A neural network gives every frame a fake score from 0% to 100%, with a memory of the frames before it.

  3. Combine and explain

    The scores become one verdict. A score between 40% and 60% is reported as "cannot decide" instead of a guess.
